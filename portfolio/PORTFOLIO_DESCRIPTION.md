## LinkedIn post

I built **Jobsite Jackpot** — a creator cash competition that runs a brand-partnership campaign as
a live game. 🎯

Paying dozens of creators by views is usually spreadsheets, DMs, and judgment calls over limited
prizes. I turned that into a self-serve product:

🏆 A live public board — countdown, cash tiers, leaderboard, "Challenger" standings, confirmed
payouts, refreshing in real time.
⚡ A strategic player decision — **Cash Out** a guaranteed prize, or **Go Overtime**, release your
spot, and play a mini-game for a bonus. A safe floor *and* a reason to keep posting.
🛠️ One operator dashboard — verify screenshots, approve payouts, catch duplicate creators, manage
a waitlist, export payouts — all from one screen.
⚖️ Fairness as code — capped tiers, first-come on open spots, a views-priority waitlist, and
automatic step-down so no qualified creator is stranded. Contested prize decisions become
deterministic and explainable instead of disputable.

The interesting engineering was in the edges: allocating scarce, capped prizes fairly; keeping a
money-game board perfectly consistent between its server render and its live refresh; and failing
closed around every payout.

Built with Python, Flask, and SQLite. I'm sharing a **standalone, interactive demo** — fictional
data, no credentials, nothing real is sent — so you can click through the whole thing locally.

## Positioning (for a portfolio page intro)

Jobsite Jackpot sits at the intersection of **creator marketing, automation, and operations
design**. It's an example of taking a messy, high-touch operational process — paying creators by
performance — and redesigning it as a product: engaging for the creators, low-effort for the
operator, and safe with real money on the line. The demo is fully interactive and built on
original, independent code with fictional data.
