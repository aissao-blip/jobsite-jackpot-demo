1. Sponsor

The sponsor of the Jobsite Jackpot Contest (the "Contest") is Toolforge Inc. ("Toolforge" or the "Sponsor"). The Contest runs in connection with the Toolforge Trades Creator Report campaign, a content activation program for brand partners of the Sponsor.

2. The Contest

Jobsite Jackpot is a performance-based content competition open to non-contracted brand partners (BPs) on the Toolforge roster. Participants post one piece of original content tied to the Toolforge Trades Creator Report, grow their views, and choose when to cash out for a payout before tier spots fill up permanently. A live leaderboard tracks standings throughout the campaign window. Advancement between payout tiers is based solely on verified view performance and the participant's own election; no element of chance determines tier payouts.

By entering the Contest, the participant ("You" or "Entrant") agrees to comply with and abide by these Rules and the decisions of the Sponsor, and You represent and warrant that You meet the eligibility requirements. You agree to accept the Sponsor's decisions as final and binding as they relate to the content of this campaign and the Contest. Entrants should look solely to the Sponsor with any questions, comments, or issues related to the Contest.

3. Eligibility

This Contest is open to those who:

  • Are legal residents of the United States or Canada (excluding Quebec)
  • Are over the age of 18 at the date of entry
  • Are active brand partners on the Toolforge roster
  • Have agreed to and are in compliance with the Toolforge Partner Program Terms and Conditions available at https://www.toolforge.example/partner-program-terms-conditions/
  • Explicitly opt in to the Contest through the submission form at jobsitejackpot.com

One entry, one Player Code, one prize per creator. Each eligible creator may submit only one contest entry, receives one Player Code, and may earn only one prize. A creator may not use multiple videos, multiple posts, or multiple social platforms to enter more than once or to earn more than one prize. Only one Cash Out per creator is permitted (see Section 6).

4. Entry Period

The Contest begins on July 21, 2026 at 12:00 am Eastern Time and ends on August 11, 2026 at 11:59 pm Eastern Time (the "Entry Period"). Only submissions completed during the Entry Period will be considered for determining eligibility and winner selection.

5. How to Enter

To participate in the Contest, eligible brand partners must:

  1. Publish one original piece of content about the Toolforge 2026 Trades Creator Report on a single platform (Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube) during the Entry Period. The content must meet the Content Requirements in Section 15.
  2. Submit that post via the official submission form at jobsitejackpot.com, including their name, email, post URL, current view count, and a screenshot of their view analytics.
  3. Receive a unique Player Code (format: JSJ-XXXX) confirming their entry.

One entry, on one platform. Each creator may submit only one contest entry, on one social platform. Duplicate entries, including the same or different content submitted again, or the same content submitted from more than one platform, will be disqualified. You are free to repost your video to other platforms for your own reach, but only the single platform you submit counts toward the Contest and only its views are tracked. Participants may submit updated view counts for their existing entry to reflect growth during the Entry Period; this updates the same entry and never creates a new one. The Sponsor reserves the right to verify all submissions.

6. The Board and Payout Tiers

As a participant's post accumulates views, they unlock cash payout tiers. Once a participant qualifies for a tier and a spot is available, they may choose to Cash Out and lock in their payout, or Go Overtime and wait to reach a higher tier for a bigger payout. Tier spots are limited; when a tier is full it is closed to new cash-outs until a spot is released (for example, when a participant Goes Overtime and gives up their spot). If the tier you qualify for is full, you may instead cash out at the next open tier below it, or wait for a spot to reopen (see Section 12). The decision to Cash Out or Go Overtime requires no payment or purchase of any kind and does not involve any element of chance.

The payout tiers are as follows:

TierViews RequiredPayoutSpots
Entry Level500+ views$150 USD10 spots

Cash Out is final and happens only once. Each creator may Cash Out one time. Choosing Cash Out locks in your tier payout, completes your participation, and ends your run. You cannot keep playing, go overtime, or earn toward another prize afterward. Going Overtime is different: it does not cash you out. It releases your current tier spot back to the pool so you can grow your views and reach a higher tier, where you may then choose to Cash Out. You are only ever paid for a single Cash Out.

The Sponsor reserves the right to adjust tier thresholds, payout amounts, and spot availability via the admin prize configuration prior to campaign launch. Any changes will be communicated to participants before the Entry Period begins.

8. The Mini-Game (Perfect Stripes)

Any participant who chooses Go Overtime from any qualified tier unlocks the Perfect Stripes lawnmower mini-game. Participants have 60 seconds to mow 100% of the virtual field. Participants who clear 100% of the field within the time limit earn a one-time $100 USD cash bonus added to their final payout, subject to the total mini-game bonus pool set out in Section 7. Bonuses are awarded in the order completions are verified by the Sponsor until the pool is exhausted. Additional completions do not earn additional bonuses. One mini-game attempt is available per Go Overtime decision; replaying or re-triggering the mini-game to claim more than one bonus per attempt is not permitted and will not be honored. The mini-game is a test of skill; outcomes are determined solely by the participant's performance and not by chance.

9. The Wildcard

Participants who submitted a valid post during the Entry Period but did not successfully cash out on any tier automatically qualify for the Wildcard at campaign close. A 24-hour Wildcard window opens at campaign close. Eligible participants may submit their final view count and a screenshot via jobsitejackpot.com for the opportunity to receive a $50 USD bonus. Valid Wildcard entries are awarded strictly in the order received; the first 4 valid Wildcard entries will win. The Wildcard pool is capped at $200 USD total.

All participants are assigned a unique anonymous alias (a color and animal combination, e.g. Red Falcon) upon submission. The public-facing leaderboard displays aliases only and never real names, emails, or identifying information. The Sponsor maintains a private record linking each alias to the participant's real identity for verification and payout purposes.

11. Verification

View counts are self-reported by participants via screenshot at time of submission and cash out. The Sponsor reserves the right to verify reported view counts against direct platform analytics where available. Submissions that cannot be verified, contain manipulated screenshots, or show evidence of fraudulent view inflation will be disqualified.

Only authentic, organic views count. Views must come from real, organic audience engagement. Paid promotion, boosting, or advertising of a contest entry (including using a platform's paid "boost" or ad tools to inflate views) is prohibited, and any views obtained that way will not count. Artificial engagement of any kind is prohibited, including bots, purchased views, view or engagement farms, click exchanges, or similar tactics. The Sponsor reserves the right to audit any entry's engagement, request platform analytics or additional screenshots, and exclude or disqualify entries showing inauthentic, paid, or manipulated views, in its sole discretion.

Final screenshots for tier cash-outs must be submitted within 48 hours of campaign close, except that Wildcard final screenshots must be submitted within the 24-hour Wildcard window described in Section 9. Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances.

12. Winner Selection and Notification

Each tier has a limited number of spots. When more eligible participants qualify for a tier than it has open spots, the open spots are awarded by highest verified view count first, with ties broken by earliest submission time. A participant whose qualified tier is full may cash out at the next open tier below it (a "step-down"), or wait for a spot in their tier to reopen. Spots reopen when a participant Goes Overtime (or is otherwise removed), and a reopened spot is offered to the highest-view eligible participant who entered earliest. Where view counts and submission times cannot be distinguished, the Sponsor reserves the right to award the spot by random draw among the tied participants, in its sole discretion. The Sponsor may confirm any cash out and award any spot at its discretion as part of payout review.

Winners will be notified within seven (7) days of campaign close via the email provided at submission. The Sponsor shall have no liability for any winner's failure to receive notices due to spam, junk e-mail, or other security settings, or for a winner's provision of incorrect or otherwise non-functioning contact information. If a selected winner cannot be contacted, is ineligible, fails to claim their prize within seven (7) days from the time award notification was sent, or fails to timely return a completed and executed declaration and release as required, the prize may be forfeited and the next eligible participant considered, as determined by the Sponsor in its sole discretion.
